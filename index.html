About Us

A Leading Trading Company Driving International Partnerships

Headquartered in Nouakchott, KUTS — KAYA Universal Trading and Services is a dynamic trading company dedicated to connecting local and regional markets with the world. We build bridges between suppliers, brands and investors through reliable international trade, brand representation and joint ventures.

Our mission is to be the partner of choice for companies seeking to expand into Mauritania and West Africa — combining deep local knowledge with a truly global outlook.

  • ✓ Reliable Import/Export Operations across international corridors
  • ✓ Trusted Brand Representation for global companies locally & regionally
  • ✓ Strategic Joint Ventures with international enterprises
